Privacy Policy

This SmartDeal Privacy Policy (the “Policy”) explains how the personal data of users of the SmartDeal platform is processed and protected. The platform consists of the smartdeal.pro website (the “Website”) and the SmartDeal mobile app (the “App”; the Website and the App together, the “Service”). The Service helps users enter into real estate transactions and register them electronically with Rosreestr (the Russian Federal Service for State Registration, Cadastre and Cartography), including through the “Mortgage Broker” feature.

1. General Provisions

1.1. Roles of the parties involved in the Service:

  • Publisher of the App on the App Store — PROFI SUPPORT LLC (“PROFI SUPPORT” MCHJ, Republic of Uzbekistan; the “Publisher”), acting on the instructions and in the interests of the Company.
  • Rights holder of the Service — Praktika Uspekha LLC (the “Company”, “we”, “us” or “our”). We determine the purposes and means of processing the personal data of users of the Service.

1.2. This Policy has been prepared in accordance with the Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan No. ZRU-547 “On Personal Data” of July 2, 2019, and other applicable laws and regulations.

1.3. This Policy applies to all personal data that we receive from users when they use the Service, including the App, and, with their consent, from third parties.

1.4. A user (“you”) is an individual who uses the Service on their own or under an agreement concluded with one of our partners to carry out and register a real estate transaction.

1.5. Processing of personal data means any action performed on it: collection, recording, organization, accumulation, storage, updating, retrieval, use, transfer, de-identification, blocking, deletion and destruction.

1.6. The Service may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for such websites; they may have their own privacy policies.

2. Personal Data We Collect

2.1. Through the App and the Website, we collect and process the following personal data (the exact set depends on the features of the Service you use):

  • Contact details: mobile phone number, email address.
  • General information: last name, first name and patronymic (including previous names, if changed), sex, date of birth, education, photo.
  • Document details: type, series and number of your identity document, date of issue, issuing authority, issuing office code, place of birth, SNILS (individual insurance account number), INN (taxpayer identification number), actual residential address.
  • Scanned documents: the pages of your identity document with your photo and with your residence registration, and your SNILS.
  • Family information: marital status, number of minor children, whether you have a marriage contract.
  • Transaction terms: type of loan, property and mortgage program; region and price of the property being purchased; amount and source of the down payment; loan amount and term; amount of maternity capital and subsidies; insurance terms.
  • Employment information: employment status and type, job title, your employer’s name, type, address and INN, the bank through which you receive your salary, length of service with your current employer, and total length of service.
  • Income information: amount of your main income, income from secondary employment, pension and total monthly income, as well as information from a document confirming your income: a 2-NDFL income certificate, a certificate in the bank’s form, a statement from the Social Fund of Russia, a self-employed income statement, a tax return, or the management accounts of a sole proprietor or business.
  • Property information: about real estate (type, area, address, value, ownership share, encumbrances, basis for acquiring ownership, legal basis for living there) and about your car (make, model, year of manufacture, value, pledge status, basis for acquiring ownership).
  • Technical data: IP address, operating system and browser type, device geolocation, internet service provider, information about your activity on the Service (date, time and number of visits, pages viewed, connection method, applications submitted).

2.2. Your photos, including the photo in your identity document, are not used to identify you. We do not process biometric personal data or special categories of personal data.

3. How We Collect Personal Data

3.1. You provide most of the data yourself in the App or on the Website: when you register, fill in questionnaires and applications, and upload scanned documents.

3.2. With your consent, we may receive your data from our partners (see Section 5.2).

3.3. Technical data is collected automatically: on the Website, using cookies (see Section 10); in the App, by the App itself and by the analytics services built into it. You can allow or deny the App access to your location and change your choice in your device settings.

3.4. Personal data is processed mainly by automated means in our databases located in the Russian Federation, including the separate applications and databases that form part of the Service. To comply with legal requirements, data may also be processed manually.

4. Why We Process Personal Data and for How Long

4.1. We process personal data only for the following purposes and only to the extent necessary for them:

  • Identifying you and giving you access to the features of the Service. Data: contact details, general information, document details, scanned documents. Retention period: for as long as our agreement with you is in effect.
  • Entering into, performing and terminating transactions involving you and third parties in your interests. Data: all data listed in Section 2.1. Retention period: until the purpose is achieved or the period established by law expires, whichever comes first.
  • Issuance of a qualified electronic signature for you by a certification authority. Data: contact details, general information, document details, scanned documents. Retention period: until the purpose is achieved.
  • Electronic registration of real estate transactions with Rosreestr, including through the “Mortgage Broker” feature. Data: all data listed in Section 2.1. Retention period: for as long as our agreement with you is in effect.
  • Transferring data to a partner you have chosen in order to complete a transaction, including through the “Mortgage Broker” feature. Data: all data listed in Section 2.1. Retention period: until the purpose is achieved.
  • Ensuring the operation and security of the Service, and preventing and investigating fraud and abuse. Data: all data listed in Section 2.1. Retention period: for as long as our agreement with you is in effect.
  • Compliance with the laws of the Russian Federation. Data: all data listed in Section 2.1. Retention period: until the purpose is achieved or the period established by law expires, whichever comes first.
  • Communicating with you: notifications, responses to your requests, and updates about the Service and our news. Data: full name, phone number, email address. Retention period: for as long as our agreement with you is in effect.
  • Informational and promotional messages about the Service, your transactions and the features of the Service. Data: full name, phone number, email address. Retention period: until the purpose is achieved, your consent expires or you withdraw it, whichever comes first.
  • Operating the Website and the App, studying how users use the Service, improving and optimizing the Service, and user profiling. Data: technical data. Retention period: until the purpose is achieved.

4.2. We do not process personal data for any purposes other than those listed in Section 4.1.

4.3. The legal grounds for processing are your consent, the agreement between you and us, processing instructions from our partners, and the federal laws governing our activities and those of certification authorities and Rosreestr.

5. Who We Share Personal Data With

5.1. We do not make users’ personal data public. We share data only with the recipients listed in Section 5.2, and only to the extent needed for a specific purpose set out in Section 4.1.

5.2. Personal data may be shared with:

  • our partners — legal entities that have entered into an agreement with us to use the Service, or an agreement with you to carry out a real estate transaction — to give you access to the Service and to perform the agreement with you;
  • certification authorities — to issue a qualified electronic signature;
  • Rosreestr — for state registration of transactions and real estate rights;
  • our affiliates (group companies) — to provide the Service;
  • analytics services — to study how users use the Service and to improve and optimize it;
  • competent public authorities — upon their reasoned request, in cases provided for by federal law.

5.3. A list of the specific companies is available at https://smartdeal.pro/third-parties.

5.4. All recipients listed in Section 5.2, except Rosreestr and competent public authorities, receive personal data under an agreement with us or on our instructions. They are required to keep the data confidential and to protect users’ personal data to a standard no lower than that set out in this Policy and required by law. Rosreestr and competent public authorities process the data they receive in accordance with the law.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

6.1. We keep personal data no longer than required for the purposes of processing (the periods are set out in Section 4.1), unless a longer period is established by law or by our agreement with you. In that case, we keep only the data needed to comply with the law or to perform the agreement.

6.2. We delete personal data:

  • within 30 days after the purposes of processing are achieved or are no longer necessary;
  • within 30 days after we receive your withdrawal of consent or after you delete your account, unless we have other legal grounds for processing;
  • within 10 business days if unlawful processing is identified;
  • when your consent expires or the retention period established by law ends.

6.3. Data is deleted from our information systems using the built-in tools of those systems so that it cannot be recovered. If another party processes data on our instructions, we ensure that it deletes the data within the same time frames.

7. How to Withdraw Consent and Delete Your Data or Account

7.1. You can delete your account directly in the App. Once your account is deleted, we stop processing your personal data and delete it within the time frames set out in Section 6, except for data we are required to retain by law.

7.2. To withdraw your consent to the processing of personal data or to request deletion of your data, simply send an email to ib@smartdeal.pro from the email address you provided in the Service, or include in your email the full name and phone number you used in the Service. If this information is not enough to verify your identity, we may ask for additional details.

7.3. You can request information about the processing of your personal data, ask us to correct it, or send us any other request:

  • by email to ib@smartdeal.pro — as an electronic document signed with an electronic signature, or as a scanned copy of a signed request.

Your request must include the details of your identity document and information confirming that you use the Service (your full name, phone number or email address), and must be signed. If the request is made by your representative, it must include the representative’s details and a document confirming their authority.

7.4. We review and respond to requests within 10 business days of receipt.

7.5. Withdrawing consent does not stop the processing of data if we have other grounds for it under applicable personal data law — for example, to perform our agreement with you or to comply with legal requirements.

8. Your Rights

8.1. Under applicable personal data law, you have the right to:

  • receive information about the processing of your personal data: confirmation that it is being processed, the legal grounds, purposes and methods of processing, the data concerned and its source, processing and retention periods, the third parties that have access to the data, and other information required by law;
  • request that your data be corrected, blocked or destroyed if it is incomplete, outdated, inaccurate, unlawfully obtained or not necessary for the stated purpose of processing;
  • withdraw your consent to the processing of personal data;
  • challenge our actions or inaction before the authority responsible for protecting the rights of personal data subjects (Roskomnadzor) or in court.

9. How We Protect Personal Data

9.1. We take legal, organizational and technical measures to protect personal data, including:

  • appointing a person responsible for organizing the processing of personal data;
  • limiting the number of employees who have access to personal data and training them on legal requirements and our internal policies;
  • storing data in databases on secure media;
  • regularly assessing risks and conducting internal audits of compliance of data processing with legal requirements;
  • protecting the systems that store data against unauthorized access, and reporting computer incidents that have resulted in the unlawful transfer of personal data;
  • other measures required by applicable personal data laws.

9.2. You must not share your account login and password with anyone else, and you are responsible for any actions taken under your account. You may not attempt to gain unauthorized access to the Service or to other users’ data, namely: guess passwords, intercept other people’s data, scan the Service for vulnerabilities, distribute malware, or deliberately exploit software bugs. If these rules are violated, we may block or delete your account.

10. Cookies and Analytics

10.1. Cookies are small text files that a website saves on your device. On the Website, cookies are used to make it work properly, to study how users use the Service, to improve and optimize it, and for user profiling.

10.2. In the App, technical data is collected for the same purposes by the App itself and by the analytics services built into it.

10.3. You can restrict or disable cookies, or delete them, in your browser settings. If you do, some features of the Website may not work properly or may become unavailable.

11. Changes to This Policy

11.1. We may make changes to this Policy. The updated version takes effect once it is published on the Service.
