How a deal goes

Tap a step to see it in the app.

A bank, developer or agency sends the package — you get a notification and see the deadline.

The electronic signature is activated inside the app. No office visit needed.

Signed documents go to registration; every stage shows up in the app.

Inside the app

Real screens: issuing your electronic signature and storage for your flat’s documents.

Questions

All questions are on the Support page

How do I contact support?
Email support@profisupport.uz or call the number on this page. We reply within one business day.
How much does it cost?
The price is shown in the app before you pay. Refund terms are in the Terms of Use.
Do I need an electronic signature?
Yes, signing requires an electronic digital signature. Support will explain how to get one.
How do I delete my account and data?
In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. Step by step — on the Delete account page.
