Delete your account

You can delete your account and data in the app itself.

privacy@profisupport.uz
  1. Open your profileIn the app, tap Profile in the bottom menu.
  2. Go to SettingsChoose Settings → Delete account.
  3. ConfirmEnter the SMS code. Your account and data are deleted within 30 days.
Important: deletion cannot be undone. Documents of completed deals are kept as long as the law requires, as stated in the Privacy Policy.

What is deleted and what is kept

Your profile, contacts and settings are deleted. Documents of completed deals are retained as required by law.

No access to the app?

Email privacy@profisupport.uz with the phone number your account is registered to.
