Delete your account

You can delete your account and data in the app itself.

support@profisupport.uz
  1. Open your profileTap Profile in the bottom menu, then tap your name at the top of the screen.
  2. Tap Delete profileThe button is at the bottom of the screen, above the menu.
  3. ConfirmIn the Delete profile? window, tap Delete. Your account and data are deleted within 30 days.
Important: deletion cannot be undone. Documents of completed deals are kept as long as the law requires, as stated in the Privacy Policy.

What is deleted and what is kept

Your profile and the electronic signature in the app are deleted, and the documents you created are no longer available on your device. Documents of completed deals are retained as required by law.

No access to the app?

Email support@profisupport.uz from the email address you gave in the app, or include the full name and phone number your account is registered to.
